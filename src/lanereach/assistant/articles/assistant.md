Open the assistant with **Ask LaneReach** in the top bar (or Ctrl+J).

## It can

- **Answer questions about your data** (the **Your data** tab): "Which hot deals do we have?", "Which deals went quiet?", "Which quotes expire soon?", "What is waiting for an answer in the inbox?", "How many replies did we get this week?", "What are our rates from Chennai to Jebel Ali?". It looks things up in your deals, quotes, contacts, rate sheets, inbox and activity (at most three lookups per question), answers with links that open the records, and shows what it looked at. Every number in the answer comes from what it looked up; when it cannot verify an answer it lists the records it found instead. It sees only your company's data and only what you may see.
- **Answer questions** about how LaneReach works (the **How LaneReach works** tab), only from these help articles and the live list of workflow steps. Each answer shows its sources. If the help does not cover something, it says so instead of guessing.
- **Draft a workflow** from your description (for example "when a contact is tagged gulf-importers, send the intro, wait 4 days and send a follow-up, then create a call task"). The draft is checked by the same validator as the canvas and saved as a **draft** for you to review, change, test-run and publish.

## It cannot

- Publish a workflow, enroll contacts, send email or change a record: you always do that yourself (the links open the records).
- Read the text of emails: it sees subjects, names and the facts LaneReach stored, not message bodies.
- Promise features LaneReach does not have.

Without an AI key configured, help answers are the best-matching help passages and data answers list the records found (shown as such). Each question counts against your company's AI budget (Settings → AI usage).
