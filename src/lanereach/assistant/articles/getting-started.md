LaneReach is an outreach platform for freight forwarders. It sends personalised emails from your agents' mailboxes to importers and exporters, reads the replies with AI, and turns interested replies into quote requests, deals and quotes for your pricing team.

## What happens end to end

1. You add **offices** and the **agents** (sender mailboxes) who sign the emails.
2. Your marketing team keeps **scripts** (Word files per language) in the scripts library.
3. You import **contacts** and their companies, for example from a CSV file.
4. A **workflow** (campaign) decides who gets which email and when: for example "tag a contact `intro` → send the intro script → wait 5 business days → send a follow-up".
5. Every email passes the **guardrails** (sending window, holidays, caps, suppressions and more) before it may leave.
6. Replies are **classified by AI** (interested, rate query, not interested, out of office…). Outreach stops on a real reply, the right person is told, and rate requests become **quote requests** with the shipment details extracted.
7. Pricing works the **pipeline** and sends **quotes** with charge lines from the deal.

## First steps

- **Settings → Services**: choose which freight lines you sell (air, ocean, road, rail, courier, customs, warehousing, project cargo).
- **Offices**: add each office with its time zone and owner.
- **Agents**: add the agents of each office, check their signature, and connect their mailbox (see *Connecting agent mailboxes*).
- **Scripts**: upload or sync the Word scripts; check the script × language matrix.
- **Countries & languages**: switch on the countries you target.
- **Contacts**: import your contacts (CSV) and tag them.
- **Workflows**: start from a starter (for example *Intro sequence*), test-run it on a contact, then publish.
