**Settings** (admins) has these tabs:

- **Sending**: the sending window (09:00–11:30 recipient time by default), the kill switch that pauses all sending, open and click tracking.
- **Services**: the freight lines you sell; menus and filters show only these.
- **Reply policy**: what happens per reply type (draft for review, forward to the owner, none) and the protection actions.
- **Branding**: logo, tagline, colours and offices used in signatures.
- **Scripts library**: SharePoint or the local folder and its sync.
- **API keys**: keys for other systems to call the LaneReach API.
- **Country calendars**: public holidays and weekends per country (used by the business-days guardrail and waits).
- **Audit log**: every change made in LaneReach, by whom and when.
