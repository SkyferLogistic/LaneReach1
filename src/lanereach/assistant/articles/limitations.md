---
slug: limitations
title: What LaneReach does not do
category: Basics
summary: Features LaneReach does not have today, so you can plan around them.
---
LaneReach does **not**:

- send messages on LinkedIn, WhatsApp, SMS or other channels: it sends email only;
- connect mailboxes through Microsoft Graph or Google OAuth yet: agent mailboxes use SMTP and IMAP with an app password;
- let the AI decide whether an email is sent, publish workflows or enroll contacts on its own;
- auto-send AI replies unless your reply policy is set to do so; by default drafts wait for a person;
- solve CAPTCHAs or scrape websites for contacts;
- book shipments or connect to carrier or TMS systems; prices come from your own rate sheets and the rates your team records, never from LaneReach itself;
- log into rate websites, store their passwords or copy prices from their pages: it opens them with the lane filled in and you record the rate you see;
- attach a PDF to a quote email: the quotation is in the email and a printable page can be saved as PDF;
- convert currencies: values are added up in the currency they were entered in;
- read mail in an agent's mailbox other than answers to LaneReach's own emails.
