The **Outbox** lists every email LaneReach sent or recorded, newest first.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Dry run | Recorded only: nothing was delivered (dry-run mode) |
| Queued | Waiting for delivery through the agent's mailbox |
| Sent | Accepted by the agent's mail server |
| Failed | Refused or undeliverable after the retries; the reason is shown |

Search by subject or address, and filter by kind (cold, follow-up, reply), agent and dates. Open an email to see it as received, its plain text, its headers and the **whole conversation**, with a link to the workflow step and its guardrail decisions.
