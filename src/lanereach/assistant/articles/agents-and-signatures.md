An **agent** is a person whose mailbox sends outreach, for example an air freight sales agent in your Dubai office.

## Service lines

Each agent belongs to one service line (air, ocean, road, rail, courier & express, customs, warehousing, project cargo) or **any**. A *Send email* step with a service line only uses agents of that line (or *any*). Agents rotate: the agent of the right line with the fewest emails today is chosen, and **the same agent keeps the whole conversation** with a contact.

## Signatures

The signature is generated from the agent's details and your branding (logo, tagline, offices, badges), or you paste custom HTML, or upload a signature picture. The drawer shows the signature as recipients see it.

## Daily cap and health

- **Daily cap**: the most emails the agent sends per day. When it is reached, emails wait until the next day (the *mailbox capacity* guardrail).
- **Active**: switch an agent off to stop sending from them (their history stays).
- **Mailbox health**: a score from bounces, complaints, flags and cap use over 30 days. A flagged mailbox does not send until the flag is cleared.

Only agents who never sent an email can be deleted; others are switched off so their conversations keep their name. Import many agents at once with a CSV file.
