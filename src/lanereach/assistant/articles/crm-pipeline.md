Every quote request opens a **deal** in the *Freight quotes* pipeline: Quote requested → Sourcing rates → Quoted → Negotiating → Won / Lost. Drag deals between stages on the **Pipeline** board.

## Deal details

Title, value and currency, service line and mode (FCL, LCL, FTL, LTL, express…), origin and destination, commodity, incoterm, cargo-ready date, weight, volume, pieces or containers, owner and expected close date. Details extracted from the reply are filled in automatically.

## Tasks and notes

Add tasks with a due date, priority and assignee; the **Tasks** page shows today, overdue and upcoming. Notes and the timeline keep the history of the deal. Workflows can create tasks, notes and deals, move deals and react to stage changes.

Moving a deal to Won or Lost publishes an event that workflows can use (for example a welcome sequence for new customers).
