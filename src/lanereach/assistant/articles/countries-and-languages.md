The **Countries & languages** page lists the countries grouped by script language, with the `{to_country}` phrase for each, a fallback, and an on/off switch.

- A contact is written to in the language of their country.
- When no script exists in that language, the contact is **held, not sent**, until the script exists (for example USA and Canada use English (North America) and are held until that script is added).
- Switch a country off to stop outreach to it.
