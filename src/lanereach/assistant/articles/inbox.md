The **Inbox** shows every conversation with contacts across all agent mailboxes.

- **Folders**: inbox, unread, awaiting reply, AI drafts, snoozed, done, sent, all.
- **Filters**: by agent mailbox, office, label or assignee; search by name, email or subject.
- **Actions**: mark read or unread, done, snooze until a date, labels, assign.
- **Reply** in the thread: your reply is sent from the conversation's agent with their signature and passes the guardrails.

Keyboard: j / k to move, e done, s snooze, u unread, r reply.
