## Signing in

- **Company sign-in** (production): "Sign in with Microsoft" uses your company's identity provider. Your administrator decides your role.
- **Development sign-in** (development only): pick a company, an email and a role. The page remembers your last choice and shows what the chosen role can and cannot do.

The chip at the top right shows who you are signed in as (email, role and company). Open it to **switch role or company** or to sign out.

## Roles

| Role | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|
| Admin | Everything: settings, agents and mailboxes, integrations, deletes, the sending kill switch | – |
| Campaign manager | Build, publish, pause and delete workflows; enroll contacts; edit contacts; CRM | Settings, agents, integrations |
| Office owner | Approve AI drafts, reply in the inbox, enroll contacts, work the CRM | Build or delete workflows, edit agents, settings |
| Pricing | Quote requests, deals, quotes and tasks; approve AI drafts | Workflows, agents, settings |
| Reviewer | Read replies, approve or reject AI drafts, reply in the inbox | Change workflows, contacts or the CRM |
| Viewer | See every screen | Change anything |

Buttons your role may not use stay visible but look locked; clicking one explains who can use it.
